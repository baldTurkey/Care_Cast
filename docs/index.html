A closer look

How Care Cast connects the dots.

The full project is designed to bring weather, health context, and messaging together. This Pages demo illustrates the flow without connecting to any services.

01

Get to know the person

A patient profile provides context such as location, preferences, and health information for more relevant check-ins.

02

Watch the weather

Weather services can provide local conditions and help identify periods when additional attention may be needed.

03

Start a conversation

WhatsApp is intended to deliver alerts and receive replies. An AI service is used in the project to help generate responses.

04

Keep checking in

A background worker and queue are designed to handle scheduled messages and follow-up monitoring.
